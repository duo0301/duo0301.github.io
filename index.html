I’m a PhD researcher in the Declarative Languages and Artificial Intelligence (DTAI) group at KU Leuven, based at the De Nayer campus. My work sits at the intersection of the semantic web and natural language processing: semantic table annotation, matching table metadata to knowledge graphs, and applying language models and graph neural networks to column annotation. My research has appeared at ICDE, ISWC and ESWC.
