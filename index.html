Photographed at Niagara Falls, May 2026

Oh hi, you found me. My name is Duo, I am a PhD researcher at KU Leuven, supervised by Prof. Anastasia Dimou, as an academic member of the Declarative Languages and Artificial Intelligence (DTAI) section of Computer Science, also working on technical solutions at the E2E core lab of Flanders Make@KU Leuven.

My interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing, tabular data and knowledge graphs, to understand and develop artificial intelligence for data integration. I am especially interested in table representation learning and the problems it raises, such as data scarcity. I also develop RAG systems for industrial use cases such as troubleshooting.

Open to job opportunities. I’m looking for a research intern position in winter/spring 2026-2027, and for full-time researcher/scientist positions starting May or June 2027.
