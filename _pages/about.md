Oh hi, you found me. My name is Duo, I am a PhD researcher at [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/kuleuven), supervised by
[Prof. Anastasia Dimou](https://www.kuleuven.be/wieiswie/en/person/00142590), as an academic
member of the [Declarative Languages and Artificial
Intelligence (DTAI)](https://dtai.cs.kuleuven.be/) section of Computer Science, also
working on technical
solutions at the E2E core lab of
[Flanders Make@KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/flandersmake).

My interests lie at the intersection of tabular
data and knowledge graphs, to understand and develop
artificial intelligence for data integration. For an overview of my PhD works, you can check
[these slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1mNJt4FjfktZF2nnLm_THIaTv0gjxqHhJYPWbi7arpZc/preview)
(7 min read). At Flanders Make, I also develop
RAG systems that support root cause analysis in industrial
use cases.
